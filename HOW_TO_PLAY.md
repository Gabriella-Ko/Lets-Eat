# How to play Let's Eat!

Six friends, one group chat, and nobody can agree on where to eat. You're the friend with the map. Read the chat, work out what everyone needs, and recommend the one place nearby that works for the whole group.

## Start playing

- **In your browser:** https://peculiarium.itch.io/lets-eat. Click **Run game**, then **Start**.
- **On your own computer:** you need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) installed. Then run:
  ```
  cd game
  npm install
  npm run dev
  ```
  and open the address it prints (usually http://localhost:5173).

Your progress saves in your browser, so you can close the game and come back later.

## How a level works

1. **Pick a level on the city map.** The map has four districts: Campus, Downtown, the Night Market and the Waterfront. Each district is one chapter, with five levels. The cards along the bottom show each level's stars and who's coming.
2. **Read the group chat.** Messages arrive one by one. Your friends say what they're craving, what they can't eat, how much they can spend and what kind of place they want. Some needs are said outright. Others are only hinted at, and some come from things that happened in earlier levels.
3. **Star the clues** (from chapter 2 onwards). Star the lines that matter and they're saved to your **Notebook** on the right, so you can check them later.
4. **Find a place.** When the chat ends, press **Find a place →**. You'll see the restaurants nearby. Open one to read its reviews: they're your evidence for what it serves and what it's like. Sort and filter the list to narrow it down.
5. **Recommend it.** Pick the place you think fits everyone, then watch how your friends react.

## Stars

| Result | Stars | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Perfect match** | 3 | The place fits every need. |
| **Partial** | 1–2 | It's safe for everyone, but someone didn't get what they wanted, such as their craving, the vibe or the price. You lose one star for each. |
| **Oh no…** | 0 | Someone can't eat there: an allergy, a diet, or a place far over budget. |

- Any result with at least 1 star unlocks the next level.
- You can replay any level for a better score. Your best score is kept.
- After a pick, **Clue recap** shows which needs you met and which you missed, with the chat lines that hinted at each one.

**Tip:** check allergies and diets first, since they're the only way to get 0 stars. Then match the craving, the vibe and the budget.

## Along the way

- **New friends.** Each friend joins the group in their own level, and their card appears the first time you meet them. You can see everyone you've met on the **Friends** page.
- **Scrapbook.** Shared memories, like the satay incident or the trip fund, become Scrapbook pages, and they come up again in later chats. A page unlocks when you win the level it belongs to.
- **Been here before.** Places you've visited in earlier levels are marked on the results list.
- **The ending.** A perfect pick on the final level starts the send-off.

## Controls

Everything works with the **mouse** alone. Everything also works with the **keyboard** alone, and every screen lists its keys at the bottom.

| Screen | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Title | **Enter** start |
| City map | **↑ ↓** pick a level · **← →** switch district · **Enter** play · **S** Scrapbook · **F** Friends · **O** Options · **C** Credits · **Esc** back to the title |
| Chat | **Space / Enter** next message · **P** pause · **A** show all · **↑ ↓** scroll, or pick a line in chapter 2 and later · **S** star the line · **Enter / →** find a place when the chat ends · **Backspace** back to the map |
| Results | **↑ ↓** choose · **Enter** open, then Enter again to recommend · **R** recommend the open place · **S** sort · **F** filter · **Backspace** close the place, or go back to the chat |
| Outcome | **← →** choose a button · **Enter** press it · **Esc** close the recap or a card |
| Scrapbook | **← →** turn the page |
| Anywhere | **M** music and volume · **Esc / Backspace** go back |

## Options

From the map, open **Options** to change:
- **Text size:** Normal or Large.
- **Chat pace:** Quick or Relaxed.
- **High contrast:** stronger colours and outlines.
- **Animations:** turn them off and things simply appear.
- **Music** and **Sound effects:** each on or off.

You'll also find **How to play** there, plus **Reset progress**, which starts the campaign over and keeps your settings.

The music button in the bottom-left corner of every screen opens the volume control.
