## Description (paste into the Description box)

---

**Six friends. One group chat. Nobody can agree on where to eat.**

*Let's Eat!* is a cozy puzzle game about the hardest question in any group chat. Read what your friends say, notice what they need (and what they don't quite say out loud), then pick the one place nearby that works for everyone.

**How to play**
- **Read the chat.** Liam wants noodles. Cyrus has a peanut allergy. Maya's saving every dollar for a trip. Some needs are said plainly, others are only hinted at.
- **Star the clues.** Save the lines that matter to your Notebook.
- **Find a place.** Sort and filter the restaurants, read their reviews, and recommend one.
- **See what happens.** A perfect pick earns 3 stars. Pick wrong and your friends will let you know.

**What's inside**
- 20 hand-checked puzzles across 4 districts: Campus, Downtown, the Night Market and the Waterfront
- 6 friends to meet, each with their own tastes, quirks and history
- A Scrapbook of shared memories that come back in later levels
- A story that follows the friends from spring to Maya's send-off
- Fully playable by keyboard, with High contrast, Large text, and options to slow the chat or turn animations off

**Controls**
- **Mouse:** click everything.
- **Keyboard:** arrows to move, Enter to choose, Esc or Backspace to go back. Each screen lists its keys at the bottom.
- **M:** music and volume.

Your progress saves in your browser.

**How it was made**
*Let's Eat!* was built for a course on AI-assisted game development. I designed the game, wrote the rules in a game bible, and approved every level. A crew of AI agents drafted the levels, chats and restaurants, and each level was checked by scripts, a critic agent and three blind playtests before it reached me. The pixel art was made with Nano Banana and PixelLab, and I picked every image. The code was written with Claude Code. Nothing calls an AI while you play.
